RE:PLAY POLICY CENTRE

One place for the rules behind Beta rewards, Creator and account data.

The product policies below describe current Beta behaviour. Formal legal Terms and Privacy wording still require final legal review before production account launch.

V1.1Rewards Policy
  • Welcome Reward: 2,000 RP.
  • Blank Cassette: 400 RP.
  • RP cannot be purchased, cashed out or transferred.
  • Creator redemption burns user RP; system issues an independent Creator Reward.
BETACreator Policy
  • Creators cannot set RP prices.
  • Asset type determines 100 / 200 / 300 / 400 RP.
  • ♡ Likes do not generate RP or alter price.
  • Creator Level and Bonus are disabled in V1.1.
DRAFTContent & Copyright
  • Uploaders must hold necessary rights.
  • Gallery needs report, review and takedown workflows.
  • Redeeming a design grants product use rights, not copyright ownership.
DRAFTPrivacy & Account Data
  • Account, reward and entitlement data will be server-managed.
  • Users need account deletion and data handling controls before production launch.
  • This public GitHub site stores no production admin credentials or user ledger.

Product-policy preview only. Legal Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are not yet represented as final legal documents.
